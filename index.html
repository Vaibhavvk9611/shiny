Government Support

The Government strengthened the Akkapade initiative by providing dedicated Bolero vehicles for every district along with trained field teams. This initiative helps in rapid response, women protection, awareness programs, and public safety support across Karnataka.

From One District to Entire State

The initiative first started in Bidar district and quickly gained recognition because of its successful field operations and women safety programs. Due to strong public response and government appreciation, the model expanded across Karnataka and became a major support system for women and children.
